# Diet Hub

Chris's lean-bulk food tracker. Open on your phone, then **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

- **Today:** say it (Wispr Flow or the keyboard mic), type it, or tap a meal; weigh each food, pick cooked or uncooked, log it. Shows calories left and which macros you've hit. Suggests amounts for your last meal or two.
- **Scan barcode:** looks the product up in Open Food Facts. If it's not there, take a photo of the Nutrition Facts label and the phone reads it.
- **Progress:** weekly check-in (suggests calorie changes from your weight trend), morning weigh-ins with a 7-day average, week-by-week averages, and past days you can add to.
- **Grocery:** the weekly haul list.
- **Ask:** free AI chat that sees today's log, your meals, the last week and your weight. Only answers on the live site.
- **Setup:** train/rest targets, your meals, your saved foods.

Everything is stored in this browser on this phone. Food numbers are USDA (SR Legacy).

Local test copy: `cd diet-hub && ./start.sh` → http://localhost:3700
